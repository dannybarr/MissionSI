## Generated for this page

These are original renders made for the Mission site. They are not photographs of products you can buy, and they are not taken from a stock library.

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `public/media/forest.webp` | A generated temperate-forest photograph used as the source for the ASCII privacy section. |
| `public/media/tray/key.webp` | A generated cut-out of a black-and-gold car key fob, standing in for an everyday-carry key. Lamborghini is a trademark of its owner; this is not an official product image. |
| `public/media/tray/airpods.webp` | A generated cut-out of a white wireless earbud case. AirPods is a trademark of Apple Inc.; this is not an official product image. |
| `public/media/tray/wallet.webp` | A generated cut-out of a brown leather card holder. |

Backgrounds were removed so the objects can drop onto the tray with CSS contact shadows.
